# Claims Register

## Status rules

- `hypothesis`: prediction from the proposal or experiment design; do not present as a result.
- `observed`: reproduced locally with retained evidence, but not yet paired with an official source.
- `verified`: paired with current official documentation, pinned version, repeatable local test,
  and retained evidence.
- `architecture/probe`: supported by pinned source/runtime inspection or a model-free probe, but not
  promoted to live-experiment behavior.
- `documented`: an implementation or contract exists, but the claimed behavior was not independently
  observed.
- `unsupported`: evidence did not support the claim; do not use.
- `gate-2c-deferred`: the shared three-framework failure/recovery experiment has not run; do not
  present framework-specific continuation or restoration evidence as that comparison.
