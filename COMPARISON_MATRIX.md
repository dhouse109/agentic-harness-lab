## Evidence-strength synthesis by organ

| Organ | Shared semantic boundary | Framework-owned observed differences | Evidence-strength note | Remaining uncertainty |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Context | Same frozen 12 target usages and permitted source/image facts | Drupal AI uses Drupal task/entity context; LangGraph refreshes shared context at nodes; CrewAI refreshes it in the model-owning Flow stage | All have accepted live evidence; privacy artifacts and retained-state shapes differ | No quality, token-efficiency, or production-scale ranking was measured |
| Tools | Same four shared semantic operations and review destination | FunctionCall adapters, LangChain `@tool`, and CrewAI `BaseTool` provide different framework-native surfaces over the same client | All three adapter paths are retained; only tested operations are in scope | Broader ecosystem size or integration breadth remains a hypothesis |
| State and memory | Completed work and recommendation identity must be recoverable without source mutation | Drupal key/value state, LangGraph `SqliteSaver` checkpoints, and CrewAI Flow/SQLite persistence have distinct ownership and schemas | Each mechanism has framework-specific local proof; those proofs are not one identical failure trial | Gate 2C process-failure behavior remains unmeasured across all three |
| Verification | Frozen output schema, deterministic validator, submission/status semantics, and no automatic source application | Each framework owns its structured-output invocation and retry controls | Accepted batches retain per-framework accounting; evidence lineage and privacy repair history are not flattened | No quality, cost, speed, security, or superiority claim follows |
| Human review | Drupal `alt_text_suggestion` remains the sole authoritative queue and generated text remains unpublished | Drupal-native revision workflow, LangGraph interrupt/resume, and CrewAI pending-Flow reconstruction/resume bridge to that same authority | Real reviewer decisions exist in each relevant framework-specific boundary; continuation mechanisms differ | No claim that one mechanism is generally safer or easier in production |
| Lifecycle and recovery | Same frozen lifecycle vocabulary and reserved target-6/7 seam | Drupal and LangGraph controlled continuation plus CrewAI-specific persistence, HTTP-failure disposition, restoration, and composite evidence governance are materially different observations | The evidence accurately supports separate descriptions, not a symmetric recovery score | Gate 2C is required before any shared failure/recovery conclusion or winner |
